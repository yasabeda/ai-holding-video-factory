A transfer sends the MP4 to the creator's TikTok inbox; the creator must open TikTok and finish editing and posting. The prepared title, description and hashtags are provided locally for the creator to apply in TikTok and are not set by this transfer. Neither a successful transfer nor a local manual status is a guarantee of publication. The application does not perform Direct Post or automatic public publishing.

Existing quality and licence gates remain required. Content that fails review must not be posted merely to meet a schedule or budget. Do not repeat an upload with an uncertain outcome until the original request and account history have been checked.

The application must not be used to bypass CAPTCHA, authentication, moderation, rate limits or other platform security controls. Platform access can be refused, limited or revoked. Disconnecting locally does not erase local history or exported packages and does not delete previously uploaded content from TikTok. Local data deletion is described in the Privacy Policy.

AI Holding Video Factory

AI Holding Video Factory is a private desktop application that its operator uses to produce videos and to publish them to the operator's own YouTube channels. It runs on the operator's own computer. It is not a public service and it does not offer sign-up to the general public.

What the application does

  • Prepares video scripts, narration, visuals, thumbnails and captions on a local computer.
  • Uploads finished videos, thumbnails and captions to YouTube channels that the operator owns and has connected.
  • Sets video titles, descriptions, visibility and scheduled publication times, and reads them back to confirm they were applied.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics reports for the connected channels (for example views and audience retention) to improve future videos.

How it uses Google sign-in

The application uses Google OAuth and the YouTube API Services. When a channel is connected, Google shows a consent screen that lists the access requested. The application requests access to: the signed-in account's email address (to confirm the correct account), uploading videos, managing the channel's videos, captions and thumbnails, and reading YouTube Analytics reports.
